# Surface: Lekka app (index.html)

Mode: Operate. Phone-first web app, one user (the owner's father), outdoors, Telugu + English.
Job: record money paid / received per work and sub-work, by voice or tap, with photo proof; see balance per work and per person.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every entry is a receipt slip torn from his own carbon receipt book: numbered, dated, signed with a name and an amount. Refuses the fintech dashboard of white cards, purple accents and donut charts.

OWN-WORLD: Carbon-ink blue shell (header, primary buttons), slip-white entries on a pale ink-tinted ground, received in deep ledger green, paid in carbon ink, words "Paid / Received" always beside the colour. Anek Telugu for Telugu and Latin, tabular rupee numerals, Indian grouping.

STORY: He opens it, sees what each work stands at, taps a work, taps the big Add button or the mic, says "Ramesh 500 ichanu", checks the filled slip, saves. Done in 15 seconds.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Ink-blue header with app name and overall balance strip (received, paid, balance); below, the list of works with their balance; fixed bottom bar: Works, People, Settings; large round Add button bottom right.

FORM: candidate 3 of 7 (carbon receipt book), seed:b7dd8cc29512cf0c (degraded roll, no challengers). Signature move: entries are numbered slips with a perforated tear edge; saving a slip tears it off the book into the list.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
